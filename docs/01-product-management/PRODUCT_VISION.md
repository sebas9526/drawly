# Product Vision

# Drawly

**Modern Raffle Management Platform**

---

# Vision Statement

Drawly aims to become the leading platform for creating, managing, and automating numbered raffles.

Our goal is to replace spreadsheets, manual tracking, paper tickets, and WhatsApp conversations with a modern, intuitive, secure, and scalable platform that simplifies the entire raffle lifecycle.

Although the first release focuses on raffle management, Drawly is being architected as a SaaS platform capable of supporting organizations of all sizes, online payments, analytics, automation, and integrations.

---

# Mission

Empower organizers with a professional platform that simplifies raffle management while providing participants with a seamless and transparent experience.

---

# Long-Term Vision

Drawly will evolve into an ecosystem where organizers can manage every aspect of their raffles from a single platform.

Future versions will support:

- Online payments
- Multiple organizations
- Team collaboration
- Mobile applications
- Analytics dashboards
- Public APIs
- Notifications
- QR Codes
- AI-powered insights
- Marketing tools

---

# Problem Statement

Today, most raffle organizers still rely on:

- WhatsApp messages
- Excel spreadsheets
- Paper tickets
- Manual payment validation
- Manual winner selection

These approaches create several problems:

- Duplicate ticket assignments
- Human errors
- Poor participant experience
- Difficult tracking
- Lack of transparency
- Time-consuming administration

Drawly eliminates these issues through automation.

# Core Values

## Simplicity

The platform should be intuitive without requiring training.

---

## Transparency

Every participant should clearly see ticket availability and raffle status.

---

## Reliability

The system should always preserve data consistency.

No duplicate tickets.

No data loss.

---

## Scalability

Every architectural decision should support future growth.

---

## Performance

The application should remain responsive regardless of raffle size.

---

## Maintainability

Code should be clean, modular, documented, and easy to extend.

---

# Product Principles

Every feature added to Drawly must satisfy these principles.

## User First

Every decision must improve the user experience.

---

## Mobile First

Most participants will access raffles from their phones.

---

## Responsive by Default

Every screen must work on desktop, tablet, and mobile.

---

## Security by Design

Security should never be an afterthought.

---

## Data Integrity

Business rules are more important than convenience.

Example:

A ticket can never belong to two participants.

---

## SaaS Ready

Even if initially only one administrator exists, the architecture must support multiple organizations.

---

# Success Metrics

The MVP will be considered successful when organizers can:

- Create raffles in less than two minutes.
- Share a raffle link instantly.
- Manage participants without spreadsheets.
- Prevent duplicate ticket assignments.
- Select winners in one click.

---

# Non-Goals for MVP

The first version will NOT include:

- Payment processing
- User authentication
- Team collaboration
- Push notifications
- Mobile applications
- Public API
- AI
- Multi-language support

These features are intentionally postponed to keep the MVP focused.

---

# Product Philosophy

Drawly is not simply a raffle application.

It is a platform designed to become the standard operating system for raffle management.

Every architectural decision must prioritize long-term scalability over short-term convenience.
